# Roadmap

## Prove the wedge

1. Run preregistered agent-alone vs prompt-loop vs Signoff experiments on held-out repositories.
2. Publish raw failures, false-signoff rate, cost, latency, and ablations.
3. Test first-run installation and mission completion with non-technical users.

## Harden proof

- isolated verification runner and private oracle boundary;
- provider- or host-attested context identities;
- mutation and verifier-challenge checks;
- explicit external/human receipts;
- artifact schema migrations and signed releases;
- calibrated claim-to-experiment selection.

## Product

- one-click coding-agent integrations;
- cleaner guided editors for Charter, Spec, Council, and Review;
- team policy and audit export;
- optional hosted isolation while preserving the open local protocol.
