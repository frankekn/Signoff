# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone. Be constructive, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, preserve dissent without hostility, and assume good intent while demanding reproducible claims.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that violate these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository contact channels. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
